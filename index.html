On the QMX, Volume sets the level to the PC as well as the headphones, so use Volume to keep the CLIP light off. The controls work once CAT is connected; each − / + is 1 dB, and the Volume slider goes no more than 20 dB above where it is now, so a slip can't blast your headphones.

Beacon bands

Detection reads the radio's Band config table (read-only) and checks each beacon frequency against your bands' min/max limits. Tick or untick to override.

Monitor

Clock check appears after a few beacons are heard.
A beacon counts as heard only when its callsign fits clearly better than any of the other 17 callsigns, at least two of its letters are confirmed on their own, and its 100 W dash follows. A beacon too weak for one slot is also looked for by stacking its last 2–8 cycles on that band (shown as ×N); this works best with “Stay on one band”, where each beacon comes round every 3 minutes. With no aerial connected, nothing should be heard.
